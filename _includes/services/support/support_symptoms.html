Why is building a team so painful?

You need senior engineering talent, but you don't have the volume for a full-time hire (or the budget for a €150k headcount). The traditional options force you to compromise.

Recruiting Drag

6-9 months to find, hire, and onboard a qualified senior architect.

Management Tax

Freelancers require you to be the PM. You spend more time managing than building.

Knowledge Leaks

When a freelancer finds a better gig, your institutional knowledge leaves with them.

Cost Variance

Hourly billing incentivizes slowness. Costs balloon with every "small tweak".
