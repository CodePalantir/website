## Why teams book this first

Most Salesforce orgs weren't designed, they accumulated. Admins came and went, consultancies layered quick fixes on quick fixes, and now nobody can say with confidence what actually happens when a lead comes in. The audit replaces that uncertainty with a document: here is what exists, here is what's fragile, and here is what each fix costs.

It's also how we earn the next engagement. Everything we propose afterwards traces back to a finding in the audit, you'll never hear "trust us" as a justification.

## How the five days run

- **Day 1**, Org access, metadata export, and a 45-minute intake call with the people who live in the system.
- **Days 2–3**, Deep read: Apex and Flow logic, data model, integrations, permissions, limits, and failure points.
- **Day 4**, Findings ranked by revenue impact and effort; every fix priced as a fixed number.
- **Day 5**, You receive the written fix-list and a 60-minute walkthrough. What you do with it, with us or without us, is your call.
